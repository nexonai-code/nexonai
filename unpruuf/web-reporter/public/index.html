This browser page gives you weaker protection than the unpruuf app: no Tor by default (your network provider can see you're talking to this relay, even though it can't read what's said), your device's normal storage keeps this conversation until you delete it below (the app instead keeps messages only in RAM and wipes them), and there's no protection against a compromised or monitored device/browser. If your organization faces retaliation risk, prefer the unpruuf Android app over this page, and consider using Tor Browser for an extra layer this page cannot provide on its own. Content is still end-to-end encrypted either way — the relay operator and network observers cannot read your report.
