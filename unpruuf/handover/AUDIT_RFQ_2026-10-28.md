## 1. About unpruuf

unpruuf lets companies and public bodies communicate and receive reports without user accounts, phone numbers or e-mail addresses. The customer runs its own server (a machine, a rented server, a container or a Windows program); NexonAI runs no servers and has no access to customer systems. Messages travel end-to-end encrypted over Tor onion services and wait on the customer's server for at most 24 hours. Three products share the system: **unpruuf Business** (internal communication, the main product), **unpruuf Compliance** (a whistleblowing channel) and **unpruuf Press** (a channel for newsrooms, a pilot). Apps are Android today; an iOS version is being built and is not part of this request.

Delivered parts: Android apps (editions standard, pro, group, client, whistleblower, press); the node server (Node.js and TypeScript, SQLite, built-in Tor, Docker image, Windows and Linux scripts, a single-file Windows program); an administration tool for the customer (unpruuf Control); the officer application and dashboard of the whistleblowing channel; a browser fallback for reporters; a licence tool and a signed release process.

## 2. What we want to be able to say afterwards

1. The node server can do exactly what its documentation says, and nothing more, under every documented setting.
2. The system holds no identities: not on the server, not in the pairing material, not in a stored packet.
3. The encryption and the key handling are sound, as designed.
4. The software a customer runs is the software that was reviewed; your report names the fingerprint of what you reviewed.

We do not expect a certificate. We expect a report with findings and evidence, a statement of what was and was not covered, and the right to quote an agreed summary.

## 3. Scope

### Base scope (Lot A): please quote this as one price

- **Node server** (source review and black-box testing): all routes, middleware and settings; storage and deletion of expired data; the owner-only write rule and the guest grants; request limits; the sealed-key scheme and the unlock route; the local setup page; the Tor integration (onion service, proof-of-work defence, bridges); the company feed; the Docker image, the Windows and Linux scripts and the single-file Windows program; what the process does on the network (a day of traffic capture on a server you control).
- **Android Business app** (editions pro, then group; the other editions share the code): static review and dynamic analysis on at least two phones, one of them rooted; storage and memory after use; traffic only through Tor; manifest, permissions, exported components; PIN, lock-out, panic wipe, screenshot protection, clipboard, notifications; inputs from outside (scanned codes, imported lists, images, files); behaviour when the phone is lost.
- **Cryptography and protocol:** review by a named specialist of the end-to-end encryption design (a ratchet-based design with an outer transport layer), session start, key derivation and storage, the use of random numbers, and the way packets are addressed for retrieval. Formal modelling is welcome if it adds value; say what you would model.
- **Network and metadata:** what a passive network observer and a server operator can learn from packet sizes, timing and addresses; assessment of the limits we declare.
- **Build and release chain:** dependency inventory (SBOM) and known vulnerabilities; the signed release manifest and the customer's way of verifying a release; how Tor is obtained and checked; reproducibility of the delivered build.

## 4. Claims to be tested

We will give the shortlisted firm a numbered list of claims (about thirty), each with its assumptions and the evidence we already have. By category: the server stores no sender address, no user agent and no identity; stored data is unreadable and removed after its lifetime of at most 24 hours; only the owner (and holders of a valid guest grant) can write; the server makes no outbound connection except the one-time download of Tor and no telemetry; the program a customer runs is the released one; the encryption design is sound; all traffic of the apps goes through Tor except one documented same-network shortcut; messages are not written to the phone's storage; the app holds no analytics, advertising or Google libraries; the protections of a lost phone work as described. The format of a stored packet may still change before the freeze (see section 9); your opinion is on the frozen version.

For each claim we expect one of three opinions with reasons: **confirmed, confirmed with reservations, not confirmed**, and where you did not test it, **not tested**.

## 5. What we expect you to deliver

1. A written report with all findings: title, severity on a named scale, description, steps to reproduce, impact, recommendation. Severity is yours; we may correct facts, never severities.
2. A **coverage statement**: for each claim, your opinion; what you did not verify and why (scope, time, access).
3. The **fingerprint** (commit identifier and file hashes) of the version you reviewed, the agreed threat model, and the tools and versions you used.
4. A **draft report** at least five working days before the final report, for factual correction by us.
5. **Interim notice** of any Critical or High finding within one working day of confirming it.
6. A **retest** in the price, covering all Critical and High findings and the Medium findings we choose to fix, on a corrected version, within ten working days of receiving it, at the latest 60 days after the final report.
7. An **executive summary** of one page without jargon, and a **short public summary written by you**, which we may show to customers. We ask for the right to quote the public summary and the coverage statement word for word, with an agreed wording, and we will not call the system "certified" or use the word "audited" without version and scope.
8. Reports in **English and Romanian** (price the second language separately if it is extra), as a signed PDF and in an editable format.
