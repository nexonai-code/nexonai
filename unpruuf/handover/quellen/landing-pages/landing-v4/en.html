5

Tor: a second internet, made for anonymity.

This is where our system lives.

Your phone operator sees everything you do.
In Tor, data hops through several points, and each knows only its neighbour.
The operator sees at most that you use Tor, never who you communicate with.
Your server is a passage, not a shelf.
Nothing stays behind. Everything is gone after 24 hours at the latest. The intelligence is on the devices, not on the server.
Why it is secure

What makes the system so secure

Not a promise, but construction decisions. Each of them takes something out of play that could otherwise be stolen.

1

There are no identities

No accounts, names or phone numbers. There is nothing that could be stolen or tied to a person.

2

There is no server of ours

The server belongs to the customer. No central point, no third party between you, nothing at NexonAI.

3

The server sees only junk

Encrypted packets, all the same size, with an expiry of 24 hours at most. Sender, recipient, names and content are not there.

4

Encrypted from the phone

The content is encrypted on the phone and only the contact can read it. The keys change by themselves.

5

Tor hides who talks to whom

Traffic runs through Tor. The server answers only through Tor, and the traffic does not show who communicates with whom.

6

Constant rhythm, sealed key

The app checks at a constant rhythm, with cover traffic, so it does not show when you write. The server's keys are sealed: after a restart, the nodes do not come back without the owner's key.

A confidential channel for whistleblowers.

For organisations with at least 50 employees that must offer an internal reporting channel (EU Directive 2019/1937, Romanian Law 361/2022).

One QR code for the whole organisation. No account, no name, no phone number.

Internal conversations that stay nowhere.

For management, teams and professions that work with sensitive information and do not want their discussions to pass through someone else's server.
