Thank you for your interest in unpruuf Compliance. So that the pilot can start once IT has approved it, we propose the framework below, including the terms after the pilot, agreed now.

# 1. The pilot
- Duration: 3 months from installation, free of charge.
- The university operates the server (own computer, virtual machine or hosting chosen by the university). NexonAI operates no servers and has no access to reports.
- Included: installation, two hours of training for the case officers, reporting page and QR code in Romanian and English, and the evidence pack for the data protection officer.
- First 30 days with test reports only; afterwards the channel can go live if the university decides so.
- A review talk every two weeks.

# 2. For the IT department
- Server requirements: a small Linux or Windows machine, always on, with internet access; no ports open to the internet.
- Case officer app: a Windows computer of the case officer.
- The evidence pack (technical overview and data map, technical and organisational measures, impact assessment template, data processing agreement template) is attached.
- We answer IT questions in writing or in a short meeting.

# 3. After the pilot
- If the university continues: setup 690 EUR once and licence with support 1,790 EUR per year, excluding VAT, for one server licence.
- The amount is below the direct award threshold under Law 98/2016 [to be confirmed by the procurement office].
- No obligation to buy. If the university does not continue, it deletes the server; no data remains with NexonAI.

# 4. Status
unpruuf Compliance is tested and works. It is not independently audited and not certified; an independent security test is planned.
