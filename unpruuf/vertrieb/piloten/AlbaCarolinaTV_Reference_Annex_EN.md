This annex supplements the pilot agreement for unpruuf Press and sets now the terms after the pilot and the use of the name as a reference.

# 1. Terms after the pilot
- If the newsroom continues after the 3 months: no setup fee and licence with support in the first year at 445 EUR (instead of 890 EUR), excluding VAT, for one server licence.
- From the second year: 890 EUR per year, excluding VAT.
- No obligation to buy. If the newsroom does not continue, it deletes the server; no data remains with NexonAI.

# 2. Reference
- NexonAI may name Alba Carolina TV as pilot newsroom for unpruuf Press, with logo, on its website and in presentations.
- NexonAI may use a short statement of the newsroom, only in the wording the newsroom approves in writing.
- The newsroom discloses nothing about sources, tips or content. The reference concerns only the use of the tool.
- Any journalistic report about unpruuf remains solely the editorial decision of the newsroom and is not part of this annex.
- The newsroom may withdraw its consent to the reference at any time by e-mail.

# 3. Status
unpruuf Press is a pilot version, not independently audited, not certified and not yet used with a real source.
