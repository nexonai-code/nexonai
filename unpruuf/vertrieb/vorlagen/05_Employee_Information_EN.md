# 1. Who is responsible
Controller: [Organisation], [address]. Data protection officer: [contact].

# 2. What the channel is for
Reporting breaches of law under Law 361/2022 and Directive (EU) 2019/1937. [Add the areas covered by your internal procedure.]

# 3. How it works
- You report through the unpruuf app or the reporting page in a browser.
- The channel runs on a server operated by [Organisation]. The software supplier, NexonAI Consulting SRL, operates no server and has no access to reports.
- No account is needed. You decide whether you give your name or other details about yourself.
- You receive a case code, an acknowledgement within 7 days and feedback within 3 months.

# 4. Which data is processed
The content you send, the case code, dates and the status of the case. Reports may contain data about other persons (persons concerned, witnesses).

# 5. Legal basis
Legal obligation, GDPR Art. 6(1)(c) together with Law 361/2022. [Confirm with the data protection officer, including special categories of data.]

# 6. Who receives the data
Only the designated case officers: [functions, not names]. Authorities, where the law requires it.

# 7. How long it is kept
Reports are kept in the register for 5 years (Law 361/2022 art. 7) [confirm in the internal procedure], then deleted. Data that is clearly not relevant is deleted without delay.

# 8. Your rights
Access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection under GDPR Art. 15–21, within the limits set by Law 361/2022 to protect the confidentiality of all persons involved. You may lodge a complaint with ANSPDCP (the Romanian data protection authority).

# 9. Confidentiality
The identity of a reporter and of persons concerned is kept confidential under Law 361/2022. Protection for reporters acting in good faith applies under the same law.
