# 2. Subject
The supplier grants the customer a licence to use unpruuf Compliance (server, case officer app, reporting app and web reporter) and provides installation, training, updates and support as described in the offer no. [number], which is part of this agreement.

# 3. Licence
- One licence covers one server of the customer. The customer may run the server at its own premises or at a hosting provider it chooses.
- The licence is non-exclusive and not transferable, for the duration of this agreement.
- The reporting app and the web reporter may be used free of charge by anyone who reports to the customer.

# 4. Operation and data
- The customer operates the server and the case officer app and is the controller for all personal data in the reporting channel (GDPR Art. 4(7)).
- The supplier operates no servers for the customer and receives no reports, case files or logs.
- If the supplier is given access to the customer's systems for installation or support, the parties first conclude the data processing agreement (template in the evidence pack).

# 5. Services
- Installation within one week after order, in a remote session started by the customer or on site.
- Training of [2] hours for the customer's case officers.
- Updates and support by e-mail at unpruuf@nexonai.ro on working days; answer within [2] working days.

# 6. Fees and payment
As in the offer: setup [amount] EUR once, licence and support [amount] EUR per year, excluding VAT. Invoices are payable within 15 days.

# 7. Term and termination
12 months from installation, renewed automatically by 12 months unless one party cancels in writing 30 days before the end. On termination the customer stops using the licence; its data stays with the customer.

# 8. Status and liability
- unpruuf Compliance is tested; it is not independently audited and not certified. The supplier does not warrant uninterrupted availability.
- The customer is responsible for its internal procedure under Law 361/2022, for its case officers and for the security of its server and computers.
- Liability of the supplier is limited to the fees paid in the last 12 months, except for intent and gross negligence and where the law does not allow a limit.

# 9. Confidentiality
Both parties keep confidential the information they learn from each other under this agreement.

# 10. Final provisions
Romanian law applies. Courts at the supplier's registered office are competent. Changes require writing. The agreement is signed in two copies, in Romanian and English; the Romanian text prevails.
