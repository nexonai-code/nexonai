# How to report
1. Install the free unpruuf app on an Android phone, or open the reporting page in a browser: [address].
2. Scan this QR code: [insert the organisation QR code here].
3. Write what happened. You do not have to give your name, phone number or e-mail address.
4. Keep your case code. With it you receive the acknowledgement and can answer questions.

# What happens next
- You receive an acknowledgement within 7 days.
- You receive feedback within 3 months.
- Your report is read only by the designated case officers of [Organisation].

# Good to know
- The app gives you more protection than a browser. A normal browser shows your IP address to the organisation's server; the reporting page tells you so.
- You may also report to the National Integrity Agency (ANI) as external channel: [ANI address / link].
- Reporters acting in good faith are protected under Law 361/2022.
