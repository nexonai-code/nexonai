# How unpruuf works
> **Prerequisite.** The customer runs its own server. NexonAI runs no servers and has no access to the customer's system, reports or case files.

unpruuf has two parts: the app and the customer's own server. Nobody has an identity in the system. No names, no accounts, no phone numbers.

Think of a mailbox. Nobody writes directly to anyone. You leave your message on your own server, and your contact picks it up from there. It works the same way in the other direction. My server, my rules.

Nothing is left lying around. What is not picked up is removed after 24 hours at most. The server is a passage, not a storage room.

- Slots: [Company], [First name], [Day 1], [Day 2]. Replace all of them before sending.
- Write to the general manager, HR or the person who handles compliance.
- Plain text, no attachments in the opening e-mail. The evidence pack goes out after the demo.
- Do not claim that anonymous reporting is mandatory. Ask about trust and confidential reporting.
- Mention fines only if the contact asks. Never as pressure.
