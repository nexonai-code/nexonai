# How unpruuf works
> **Prerequisite.** The customer runs its own server. NexonAI runs no servers and has no access to the customer's system, reports or case files.

unpruuf has two parts: the app and the customer's own server. Nobody has an identity in the system. No names, no accounts, no phone numbers.

Think of a mailbox. Nobody writes directly to anyone. You leave your message on your own server, and your contact picks it up from there. It works the same way in the other direction. My server, my rules.

Nothing is left lying around. What is not picked up is removed after 24 hours at most. The server is a passage, not a storage room.

Questions to ask during the demo:
1. How do reports reach the company today?
2. Who will read the reports in your company? How many case officers?
3. Where can the server run: on a computer in the company or at a hosting provider?
4. Is there a DPO or a lawyer who should see the evidence pack?

Honest points to state when relevant:
- unpruuf Compliance is tested and works. It has not been independently audited and is not certified. An independent security test is planned.
- Reporting from a normal browser shows the IP address to the customer's own server. The browser page says so on its opening screen. The app does not have this limit.
- There is no password recovery for the case officer. A lost password means the case files cannot be opened.
- The architecture supports data minimisation, storage limitation and confidentiality (GDPR Art. 5, 25, 32). The customer is the controller. The DPO or a lawyer confirms.

# Trial (30 days)
What we set up:
- The server at the customer or its hosting provider.
- The case officer app on a Windows computer.
- The reporting page and QR code in Romanian and English.
- A short introduction for the future case officers.
- The evidence pack for the DPO.

Before any remote session or on-site work with access to the customer's systems, the DPA is signed.

Success criteria at day 30:
- A test report sent through the app and one through the browser arrived at the case officer.
- The case officer acknowledged a test report and the reporter received a case code.
- A question and an answer went back and forth in the same case.
- The overview shows the deadlines for each test case.
- A test case was exported and a test case was deleted.
- The case officer opens the app once a day as a routine.
- The DPO has received the evidence pack and has no open blocking questions.
- The team at the customer has decided: offer yes or no.

# Trial checklist
1. Date for the trial start agreed.
2. Server location chosen: computer in the company or hosting provider.
3. Windows computer for the case officer ready.
4. DPA signed, if we get access to the customer's systems.
5. Server installed (remote session started by the customer, or on site).
6. Case officer app installed, officer password set and kept safe. No recovery possible.
7. Reporting page and QR code in RO and EN tested.
8. Test report through the app.
9. Test report through the browser.
10. Acknowledgement sent, case code received.
11. Question and answer in the same case.
12. Export and deletion of one test case.
13. Evidence pack sent to the DPO.
14. Check after 2 weeks: open questions.
15. Decision call at day 30, offer sent the same week.

# Check-in after 2 weeks
1. Does the case officer open the app every day?
2. Are the workplace notice and the employee information published?
3. Are there open questions about deadlines, replies, deletion or export?
4. Did the DPO raise any points?
5. Would you agree to be named as a reference for other companies? A name and one sentence are enough. We never ask about cases; we have no access to them.
