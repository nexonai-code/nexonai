# How unpruuf works
> **Prerequisite.** The customer runs its own server. NexonAI runs no servers and has no access to the customer's system, reports or case files.

unpruuf has two parts: the app and the customer's own server. Nobody has an identity in the system. No names, no accounts, no phone numbers.

Think of a mailbox. Nobody writes directly to anyone. You leave your message on your own server, and your contact picks it up from there. It works the same way in the other direction. My server, my rules.

Nothing is left lying around. What is not picked up is removed after 24 hours at most. The server is a passage, not a storage room.

## Opening
"Good morning, [First name]. This is NexonAI Consulting from Alba Iulia. I am calling about the internal reporting channel under Law 361/2022. Do you have two minutes?"

If not: "When may I call again?" Note the time and end politely.

## Two questions
1. "If an employee at [Company] wanted to report a problem today, confidentially, how would they do it?"
2. "And who reads those reports and keeps track of the deadlines: confirmation within 7 days, feedback within 3 months?"

Listen. Note the answers in the contact record. They decide how the demo starts.

## Value in two sentences
"unpruuf Compliance is a reporting channel that runs on your own server, without names and without accounts, and we have no access to it. Your case officer sees every case with its next deadline, and we install it, train your people and give your DPO the documents they need."

## The ask
"I would like to show you this in 20 minutes online. Would [Day 1] at [time] or [Day 2] at [time] suit you better?"

After a yes: confirm by e-mail the same day, with the date and the link.

# Answers to objections
## "We already have one."
"Good, then you know the topic. May I ask how reports reach you today: e-mail, phone, a box, a form? And do people actually use it?"

If the answer is a shared mailbox or a phone number: "Then the reporter is usually visible. With unpruuf Compliance the report arrives without a name, and the reporter can still answer questions through a case code. The case officer sees the deadlines in one overview. In 20 minutes you can compare it with what you have."

## "It is too expensive."
"That is fair to check. Romanian cloud tools are often cheaper. They run on the provider's servers, so the reports are stored with the provider. With us, the channel runs on your own server, and we have no access. Installation, a 2-hour training for case officers and an evidence pack for your DPO are included. International tools cost about as much as we do."

"You can test it free for 30 days before you decide."

## "Is it certified?"
"Law 361/2022 does not require any certification for a reporting channel. unpruuf Compliance is tested and works. It has not been independently audited and it is not certified. An independent security test is planned. Until then, your DPO receives an evidence pack: technical overview, technical and organisational measures, a DPIA template and a DPA template. Your DPO or lawyer makes the assessment for your company."

## "We have no IT."
"You do not need an IT department. You need a server: a computer in the company or a hosting provider. We install the server and the case officer app, in a remote session that you start, or on site. The case officer app runs on a Windows computer and should be opened once a day. If we get access to your systems for the installation, we sign a data processing agreement beforehand."

## "We do not need it."
"Understood. One question before I let you go: if an employee had a serious concern tomorrow, where would they take it?"

If there is no clear answer: "Law 361/2022 asks companies with 50 or more employees for an internal channel. The 30-day trial costs nothing and shows whether it fits. May I send you a short e-mail with the details?"

If they are firm: thank them, end the call, no further follow-up.

# Facts at hand
- Law 361/2022: internal channel mandatory for private employers with 50 or more employees (art. 9); for 50–249 employees since 17.12.2023 (art. 36). Directive (EU) 2019/1937.
- Acknowledgement within 7 days, feedback within 3 months (art. 10).
- Reports kept in a register for 5 years (art. 7).
- ANI is the external channel and the sanctioning authority.
- Fine for a missing internal channel: 3,000–30,000 RON (art. 28(2)(c)). Only if asked. Never as pressure.
- The law does not require certification of reporting channels.
- Do not say that anonymous reporting is mandatory in Romania. The status is unclear.
- Known limit, say it when relevant: reporting from a normal browser shows the IP address to the customer's own server. The browser page says so on its opening screen. The app does not have this limit.
