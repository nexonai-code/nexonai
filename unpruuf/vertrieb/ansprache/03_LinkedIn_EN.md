# How unpruuf works

unpruuf has two parts: the app and the customer's own server. Nobody has an identity in the system. No names, no accounts, no phone numbers.

Think of a mailbox. Nobody writes directly to anyone. You leave your message on your own server, and your contact picks it up from there. It works the same way in the other direction. My server, my rules.

# Post A: What Law 361/2022 asks of your company
If your company has 50 to 249 employees, Law 361/2022 has applied to you since 17 December 2023.

What it asks, in short:

- An internal channel where employees can report wrongdoing.
- An acknowledgement to the reporter within 7 days.
- Feedback within 3 months.
- A register of reports, kept for 5 years.

ANI is the external channel and the authority that checks.

The law does not ask for a certified tool. It asks for a channel that works and is used. That second part is the harder one. People report when they trust the way in.

How do people in your company report a concern today?

# Post B: Why reporters do not trust an e-mail mailbox
Many companies set up a shared address as their reporting channel. Something like compliance@company. On paper, the job is done.

Then nobody writes.

Not because nothing happens. Because the reporter sends from their own address. Their name is in the message. They do not know who else reads the inbox, or where the messages are stored.

A reporting channel is only as good as the trust in it. People ask themselves one question before they write: will this come back to me?

A channel earns trust when the reporter does not need to give a name, does not need an account, and still gets an answer.

What would make your colleagues write?
