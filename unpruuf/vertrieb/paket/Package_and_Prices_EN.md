# 1. What you get
- Your own reporting channel: people report from the free app or from a browser, without a name, and follow up with a case code.
- Installation of the server at your organisation or at your hosting provider, and of the case officer app on the case officer's computer. We work in a remote session that you start, or on site.
- A reporting page and the QR code of your organisation, in Romanian and English.
- Two hours of training for your case officers: receiving a report, acknowledgement within 7 days, dialogue, feedback within 3 months, closing, deleting and exporting a case.
- Templates: notice for the workplace and information for employees.
- Evidence pack for your data protection officer: technical overview and data map, technical and organisational measures, pre-filled impact assessment template, data processing agreement template.
- One year of updates and support by e-mail.

> **Prerequisite.** Your organisation operates the server. NexonAI operates no servers and has no access to your reports.
