# 1. About this template
An internal reporting channel processes information about possible breaches and about the people involved. Many supervisory authorities consider such channels likely to require a data protection impact assessment. The parts that describe unpruuf Compliance are pre-filled. Fields marked [ ] are completed by your organisation together with its data protection officer. This template is not legal advice.

# 2. Description of the processing
| Item | Content |
| Controller | [ ] Organisation name, address |
| Data protection officer | [ ] Contact |
| Purpose | Receiving, handling and following up reports under Directive (EU) 2019/1937 and Law 361/2022 |
| Legal basis | Legal obligation, GDPR Art. 6(1)(c) together with Law 361/2022; for special categories in a report: GDPR Art. 9(2)(g) or (b) [ ] confirm |
| Data subjects | Reporters, persons concerned by a report, witnesses, case officers |
| Data categories | Content of reports and replies, attachments, case code, dates, case status. The software collects no name, account, phone number or e-mail of the reporter. |
| Recipients | Designated case officers; authorities where the law requires it |
| Systems | Reporting app or web reporter, the organisation's own server, case officer app on a Windows computer (see Technical overview) |
| Retention | Reports kept 5 years in the register (Law 361/2022 art. 7) [ ] confirm against the consolidated text and the internal procedure |
| Transfers outside the EU | None by the software. [ ] Confirm the server location. |

# 3. Necessity and proportionality
- Only the content a reporter chooses to send is processed; no account or contact data is required.
- The server holds encrypted packages for at most 24 hours; case files exist only on the case officer computer.
- Single cases can be deleted when the retention period ends; a deletion log without content documents it.
