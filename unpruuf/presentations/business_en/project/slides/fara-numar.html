How it works

Never give out your phone number again

You share a code. Delete the contact, and there is no way to you any more.

A code, not a number

Every contact gets their own code. No phone number, no email, no WhatsApp, no shared address book.

One code per person

From the codes alone, two contacts cannot be linked. Whoever holds one learns nothing about your other contacts.

Delete, and it is over

Your phone stops collecting from that person. There is no way to you any more, unless you hand over a new code.

What someone has already received stays with them. This closes the unpruuf path, not your other channels.
