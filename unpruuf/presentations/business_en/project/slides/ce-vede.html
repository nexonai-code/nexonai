The node

What the node sees, and what it does not

Sees

Encrypted blocks of fixed size under numbers that change every hour. Deletes them itself after 1 to 24 hours.

Does not see

The content. The sender. The recipient. The IP address. Who belongs to whom.

A seized or stolen server contains nothing readable and only runs again after a restart once your app releases it.
