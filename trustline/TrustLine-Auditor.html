TrustLine Offline Auditor
Works without any connection to TrustLine. Nothing is uploaded.

Verify exported logs

Load the log export of one or both network operators (trustline-log-OP-….json) and, if available, the relay log (trustline-relay-log.json). The tool recomputes every hash, checks every signature and compares the logs with each other. The verification method is published, so you do not have to trust TrustLine.
